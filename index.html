📚 Learn to recognize common scams

💰 Fake Prize / Lottery

Unexpected big reward + “pay a small fee / tax / processing charge to claim”. Real prizes never ask you to pay first.

💼 Fake Job Offers

Very high salary for little work + registration / training / kit fee. Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay.

🏦 Fake Bank / KYC

“Account will be blocked today – verify KYC now” + external link. Banks never ask for OTP, PIN or password via SMS/link.

📦 Fake Delivery

Unexpected parcel + small payment (₹1–₹50) to “reschedule / release”. Real couriers don’t demand payment via random links.

❤️ Romance / Emergency Money

Fast emotional connection + sudden emergency + urgent request for money. Never send money to someone you only know online.

🔗 Phishing Links

Lookalike domains (paypa1.com, bankofindia-secure.in), missing HTTPS, or strange paths. Always type the official address yourself.

Important: ScamShield gives a risk assessment, not absolute certainty.
